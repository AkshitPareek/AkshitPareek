### Hey, I'm Akshit 👋

I write code that lives close to the silicon. Day job: optimizing ML inference kernels on TI's C7x DSPs, vectorizing operators, squeezing out cycles, chasing numerical stability through BF16 and FP8. There's something deeply satisfying about making a softmax 3× faster.

Before kernels I spent a couple of years deeper in embedded: multicore traction inverters on ARM Cortex-R, power-line comms on C2000, software SPI over GPIO at 10 MHz because the hardware peripheral wouldn't cooperate. The problems I like best sit right at the boundary between math, hardware, and "what if we just tried it."

### How I got here

I got into computers through gaming, modding, and scripting before I had reliable internet at home. That led to Linux, networking, Wi-Fi security, and eventually building things: Scratch games, batch files, HTML sites, a PHP cryptic-hunt platform that 1000+ people actually used. Somewhere along the way I picked up the only operating principle I've kept: if something should exist, just build it.

### What I'm into lately

Robotics and simulations, fleet control and edge-cloud inference, SIMD intrinsics, low-precision number formats (FP8 e4m3 is a beautiful little spec), build systems that don't make me cry, small Rust side projects, and watching a tricky math problem dissolve into a tight inner loop.
